ArchRavels is a competitive board game set in the world of fiber arts. Play as one of 8 characters, each with their own crafting specialty. Hit the Yarn Bazaar to build up your stash, follow the patterns to make cuddly bears, warm blankets and cozy scarves, then turn in what you finish to score. Most points wins. 2 to 4 players, 30 to 45 minutes, ages 8+.
